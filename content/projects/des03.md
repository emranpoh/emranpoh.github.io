## About

A teaser campaign for Cold Brew Trio, focusing on creating engaging visual content and branding materials to introduce the product line.

## Project Scope

- Teaser campaign design
- Visual identity development
- Marketing collateral creation
- Social media assets

## Design Philosophy

The project aimed to:
- Create anticipation and excitement for the product launch
- Develop a cohesive visual language that reflects the brand
- Engage target audiences through compelling design
- Establish a memorable brand presence
