## About

A collaborative branding project between Geylang Drip City (GDC) and HDC, combining creative vision and strategic design to create a unique brand identity that represents the partnership.

## Project Scope

- Collaborative brand identity development
- Visual design system creation
- Marketing collateral design
- Brand guidelines documentation

## Design Philosophy

The project aimed to:
- Blend the distinct identities of both brands
- Create a cohesive and memorable collaborative presence
- Reflect the shared values and vision of the partnership
- Appeal to contemporary urban audiences
